Day one
The invitation Day one — the invitation

A friend invited me to a new calisthenics class in my city. Just come and play, he said. So I went to play.

I was confident. Very strong in pushing, coming from a dancing background. I had nothing to worry about.

Halfway through the class: "OK guys, let's do some pull-ups."

I grabbed the bar, squeezed with everything in my palms, and — PULL! My back switched on. And I couldn't even get halfway.

"Wait, that's not possible. One more time."

Grasped the bar again, squeezed firmly, up — zero. Nada. Caput. Halfway again, and only with one arm. The other didn't even try.

The hole The hole

I was so embarrassed, so humbled, so reality-checked, that I stopped talking and fell into a deep black mental hole.
