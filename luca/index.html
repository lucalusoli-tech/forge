The point of all this

Be your hero.

Don't go through what I went through. Learn straight away what you actually need to crack your goal, in the shortest amount of time.

Method

Foundations first. Every time.

Most people don't fail because they're not working hard enough. I was proof of that. They fail because they're working hard at the wrong level, with no way to tell whether anything is improving.

01 Assess

Find the level you're actually at

Before anything gets programmed, I test what your shoulders, scapula and grip can currently hold. Not what you think you can do. What holds up under load.

02 Foundation

Build the thing the skill sits on

Grounded in Overcoming Gravity principles. The muscle-up, the handstand, the front lever are outcomes of positions you can hold and control. Train the positions and the skills arrive on their own.

03 Progress

Every session has a standard

You know what you hit last time and what you're chasing this time. Progression is written down, not guessed at. This is the part I never had when I was alone.

04 Recover

Train in a way you can repeat tomorrow

Two sessions a day for six months got me nothing but pain. Volume you can absorb beats volume that impresses you. The people who progress are the ones still training in year two.

Load is the last thing you add, not the first thing you chase.

Working together

Two ways in.

Both are 1-on-1 with me. Both run on a three month minimum, because that is roughly how long it takes for foundations to turn into something you can see.

Programming built for your level, your equipment and your week. Video review on the movements that matter, so the correction still happens, just asynchronously.
